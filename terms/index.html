What the service does

We write an Arabic advertisement for your business, prepare its image, publish it on your Facebook Page as a Facebook and Instagram advertisement, and send you a report when it finishes. The advertisement is paid for through our own advertising account, and you pay us by local money transfer.

What you are responsible for

  • Your Page. You must own or be authorised to advertise on the Facebook Page you connect.
  • What you tell us. The description of your business must be accurate. We advertise what you tell us you sell.
  • Your own legality. Your business and the goods or services you advertise must be lawful.
  • Replying to customers. The advertisement sends people to your Messenger or Instagram inbox. Answering them is your job, not ours.

What we will not advertise

We refuse advertisements for categories Meta prohibits or restricts, including alcohol, tobacco, weapons, drugs, gambling, adult content, political content, and anything unlawful. We also refuse anything misleading. If we refuse your order before it runs, you are refunded in full.

Approval and payment

  • You see the advertisement text and image and approve them before anything is published.
  • You pay by money transfer. The advertisement is published after the payment is confirmed.
  • The price you are quoted is the price you pay for that order. A later change to our prices does not change an order already placed.

What we cannot promise

We cannot promise a number of customers, messages, sales or any particular result. Advertising performance depends on your business, your prices, your photos and how you answer people who contact you. We promise to publish the advertisement you approved, for the budget and the number of days you paid for, and to report honestly what it achieved.

Meta may reject or stop an advertisement under its own policies, and that decision is not ours. If an advertisement is rejected before it spends, you are refunded. If it is stopped partway, you are refunded the unspent part.

Cancelling

You can cancel before the advertisement is published and be refunded in full. Once it is running it cannot be cancelled, because the budget has been committed to Meta, but you may ask us to stop it and we will refund whatever has not been spent.

Your data

How we handle your information is set out in our privacy policy, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time — see how to delete your data.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Orders already placed keep the terms that applied when they were placed.
