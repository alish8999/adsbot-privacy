Flamingo Media Ads Bot

A Telegram bot that helps small businesses in Syria create and run Facebook and Instagram advertisements — entirely in Arabic, without an ad account, a card, or any experience of advertising.

Telegram: @spark_MeBot · Operated by Flamingo Media

How it works

  1. The shop owner messages the bot and describes their business in one message, in Arabic.
  2. The bot writes the advertisement text and prepares the image. They can ask for changes until they are happy with it.
  3. They choose a city to target, a budget, and how many days to run.
  4. They connect their own Facebook Page, which they keep full ownership of and can disconnect at any time.
  5. They pay locally by money transfer, because they cannot pay Meta directly.
  6. When the advertisement finishes, they get a short report in Arabic explaining what they got for their money.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This page explains what the service collects, why, and who else sees it.

What we collect

  • Your Telegram account id and first name, so the bot can recognise you between messages.
  • What you tell the bot about your business — the description you write, the city you choose, the budget and the number of days.
  • Images you send, if you upload a photo for your advertisement.
  • Your payment receipt — the photo of the transfer and the reference number you type, so the transfer can be confirmed.
  • Your Facebook Page — its id and name, so the advertisement can be published on it.

The bot does not ask for, and does not store, your card details. Payment is made outside the bot by money transfer.

Why we collect it

Only to do what you asked: to write your advertisement, create the image, publish the advertisement on Meta, confirm your payment, and report back how the advertisement performed.

Who else sees it

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) — receives your advertisement text, its image, the city you are targeting and your Page id, because that is where the advertisement runs.
  • Anthropic — receives your business description and your Page name, to write the advertisement text.
  • Google — receives your business description and any photo you upload, to create or improve the advertisement image.
  • Telegram — delivers the bot's messages to you, including your advertisement and its results.

Your information is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Order records, receipts and advertisement results are kept as a business record of work that was paid for. Images are kept while the advertisement is running and for the record afterwards.

Your choices

You can ask for your information to be deleted, or ask what is held about you, by sending a message to @spark_MeBot on Telegram. See how to delete your data. Records required to keep proof of a paid order may be retained.

Children

This service is for business owners and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date above.
