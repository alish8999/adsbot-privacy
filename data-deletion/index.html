How to delete your data

Flamingo Media advertising bot on Telegram (@spark_MeBot) · Last updated 3 November 2026

You can ask for everything the bot holds about you to be deleted. There is no form to fill in and no account to log into — you ask in the same place you used the bot.

Steps

  1. Open Telegram and go to the chat with @spark_MeBot.
  2. Send the message delete my data (in English or Arabic — احذف بياناتي works too).
  3. You will get a reply confirming the request.
  4. Your data is deleted within 30 days, and you get a message when it is done.

If the bot is not responding, the request can also be made by replying to any message the bot has sent you.

What gets deleted

  • Your Telegram account id and name.
  • Everything you told the bot about your business.
  • Every image you uploaded, and every image the bot generated for you.
  • Your advertisement texts and your conversation history with the bot.
  • Your Facebook Page details held by the service.

What is kept, and why

If you paid for an advertisement, the record of that payment — the amount, the date and the reference number — is kept as proof that the work was paid for and delivered. This is a normal accounting record and is required to settle any dispute about money. It is kept separately from your advertisement content, which is deleted as described above.

Advertisements already published on Facebook or Instagram are removed from Meta as part of the deletion. Anything Meta keeps in its own records is governed by Meta's privacy policy, not by this service.

What we cannot delete for you

Two things are outside our reach, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise:

  • Messages already delivered through Telegram. Your advertisement image and your payment receipt were sent to us as Telegram messages, so copies exist in that chat and on Telegram's own servers. We delete our copies; we cannot delete Telegram's.
  • Your own copy of the conversation. The chat on your phone belongs to you. You can clear it yourself in Telegram at any time.

Deleting only one order

If you want one advertisement removed rather than everything, say which one in your message to the bot and only that order is deleted.

فيك تطلب حذف كل شي عندنا عنك. ما في استمارة ولا حساب، بس ابعتلنا رسالة من نفس المكان اللي استخدمت فيه البوت.

  1. افتح تيليغرام وروح عالمحادثة مع @spark_MeBot.
  2. ابعت رسالة: احذف بياناتي.
  3. رح يوصلك رد يأكد الطلب.
  4. منحذف بياناتك خلال 30 يوم، ومنبعتلك رسالة لما تخلص.

شو منحذف؟ رقم حسابك واسمك، وكل شي حكيتلنا عنه عن مشروعك، وكل الصور اللي رفعتها أو جهزناها إلك، ونصوص إعلاناتك ومحادثتك مع البوت، ومعلومات صفحتك.

شو منحتفظ فيه؟ إذا دفعت لإعلان، منحتفظ بسجل الدفعة (المبلغ والتاريخ ورقم الحوالة) كإثبات إنه الشغل تم ودُفع. هاد سجل محاسبي عادي ومنفصل عن محتوى إعلانك اللي منحذفه.

شو ما منقدر نحذفه؟ الرسائل اللي بعتها عبر تيليغرام — صورة إعلانك وإشعار الحوالة — في منها نسخ عند تيليغرام نفسه. منحذف نسخنا نحنا، بس ما منقدر نحذف نسخة تيليغرام. والمحادثة اللي عندك على موبايلك ملكك إنت، وفيك تمسحها من تيليغرام وقت ما بدك.
